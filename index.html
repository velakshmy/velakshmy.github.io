FAQ

Common questions

What is AI search optimization?

It means making your business easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews to find, understand and recommend. People also call it GEO or AEO. It builds on good SEO, it doesn't replace it.

Do you only work with businesses in Texas?

No. I'm based in Dallas–Fort Worth and know Texas markets well, but I work with businesses and teams anywhere in the US & Canada, all remotely.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Mostly local and service businesses, like home services, contractors, clinics and med spas. I also consult for agencies and in-house marketing teams at larger brands.

How long until I see results?

Quick fixes, like technical issues or your Google Business Profile, can help within weeks. Bigger gains in rankings and AI mentions usually take 3 to 6 months. I'll be honest about timing in our strategy session.

Why is the strategy session paid?

Because it's real work, not a sales pitch. It's $197 for 45 minutes. We brainstorm ideas for your business and you leave with clear next steps you can use either way. If you decide to work with me, the full fee comes off your first invoice.
